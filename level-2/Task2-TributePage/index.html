HIS LIFE STORY

A Life of Inspiration

Dr. Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam was an Indian aerospace scientist, author, teacher, and the 11th President of India. Born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu, he rose from humble beginnings to become one of India's most respected leaders.

He played a significant role in India's civilian space programme and military missile development. His dedication to science, education, and young people earned him the affectionate title "People's President."
